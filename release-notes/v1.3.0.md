LMusic 1.3 — Spaces: lofi, focus, a listening garden, parties with friends and a lot more joy.

### New Spaces (Home › Spaces)
- **Lofi:** a lofi generator made on your phone (mood, tempo, key, swing, chord colour, crackle, rain, tape wobble, warmth, drums and melody), lofi radio from public stations, and an ambience layer (rain, café, fireplace, waves) you can play under any song.
- **Focus:** a focus timer with a calm mix, instrumental songs, lofi or silence, and a soft chime for breaks.
- **Listening garden:** a plant that grows on the days you listen. It never wilts. Seasons, milestones at 7, 30 and 100 days, a shareable card and a home-screen widget.
- **Party queue:** friends on the same Wi-Fi scan a code, search your library and add songs. Votes decide what plays next, you can approve requests, and there's a TV view and a summary when the party ends.
- **Taste match:** show your taste code, scan a friend's and see your match %, shared artists and songs you'd love from each other. Works offline.
- **Cover studio:** pattern covers in LMusic colours for songs without artwork, saved into the files.

### Player and everyday use
- **Living artwork:** the now-playing cover gently breathes in time with the song. Double-tap the cover to like.
- **Artist story:** every artist page shows your history: first play, hours, your song, peak month and a timeline.
- **Mini player:** swipe left or right for next and previous, down to dismiss.
- **Swipe between pages:** Home, Library, Search and Stats. The bottom bar is slimmer.
- **Stronger haptics** everywhere, with a new Strong level (Settings › Behavior › Haptics).
- **Gestures (off by default):** shake to shuffle, turn face-down to pause (Settings › Behavior › Gestures).
- Continue listening is gone from the top of Home.

### Reliability
- If LMusic ever crashes, the next launch offers to report it on GitHub with the details filled in. After repeated startup crashes it opens in safe mode.
- Every build is now installed and walked through on an Android emulator before release.
