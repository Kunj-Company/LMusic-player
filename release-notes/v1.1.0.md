LMusic 1.1 — a full redesign. Installs as an update over 1.0.

**Install:** download `LMusic-1.1.0.apk` below on your phone and open it.

### What's new
- **New look everywhere:** flat white and ink surfaces, Bricolage Grotesque headlines, Figtree text, square album art with colour placeholders.
- **The player follows the album:** every song's artwork sets the accent: the background tint, seek bar, play button and controls. Songs without artwork use Ember orange.
- **New Home:** a greeting, Your mix with one-tap play, Recently played covers and This week's listening (time, plays, top artist, the song on repeat).
- **Stats tab:** Stats moved into the bottom bar (Home · Library · Search · Stats), with range chips for week, month, year, lifetime and custom.
- **Song rows, mini player, bottom bar and Settings** redesigned to match.
- **New About page.**

Nothing was removed: everything from 1.0 is still there.
