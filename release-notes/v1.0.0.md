First LMusic release — PixelPlayer's player with a Yuyu look, a tag editor that writes rich metadata into your files, autofill, and deeper listening stats. Android 15 and newer.

**Install:** download `LMusic-1.0.0.apk` below on your phone and open it. Allow installs from your browser or My Files when Android asks. Future releases install as updates over this one.

### New in LMusic
- **Yuyu design:** Yuyu colors (light and dark), Bricolage Grotesque + Figtree type, new app icon. The player still picks up colors from the album art. Switch back to wallpaper colors in Settings → Appearance → App colors.
- **Tag editor:** album artist, year and composer join title, artist, album, genre, track, disc, lyrics, ReplayGain and cover art — all written into the audio file (MP3, FLAC, M4A/AAC).
- **Autofill:** Edit song → Autofill. Offline guesses from the filename and folder, plus MusicBrainz matches with a confidence score and Cover Art Archive covers. Choose which fields to take, then Save. No account or API key.
- **Analytics:** plays count after 30 seconds; pick week, month, year, all time or a custom date range; new skip rate, new songs and new artists insights; history kept for life.

### Kept from PixelPlayer
Queue, shuffle, repeat, playlists, gapless playback, sleep timer, folders, search, lyrics, equalizer, crossfade, widgets, Chromecast, Android Auto, Wear OS, backups and online sources.
