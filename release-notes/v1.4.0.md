> [!TIP]
> **Installing this update keeps everything.** Download `LMusic-1.4.0.apk` below on your phone, open it and tap **Update**. Your library, playlists, listening stats, settings and edited tags stay exactly as they are.

LMusic 1.4 — noise, sleep, a screensaver, new widgets and a lot of fixes.

### New
- **Noise in Focus:** white, pink, brown, grey, blue and violet noise, plus fan, rain, ocean, stream, wind, fireplace, café, train and airplane cabin. Mix up to three, shape the tone, save presets, play it under your music, or use optional binaural tones with headphones.
- **Sleep space** (Home › Spaces): noise, lofi or a calm mix with a sleep timer that slowly lowers the volume, and a dim clock screen.
- **Screensaver:** when the phone is charging, playing music and turned to landscape, LMusic offers a screensaver with the time, date, weather and the current song. It's made for OLED screens: pure black, dim text, and everything drifts slightly so nothing burns in. Settings › Appearance › Screensaver. Also available as the system screen saver.
- **Quick Settings tiles:** Focus, Lofi and Noise.
- **Widgets:** six new ones — Now playing, Album art, Queue, Lofi & Focus, Stats and Recently played — plus a compact player for the lock screen (on phones that allow lock-screen widgets from apps, like One UI 8). Like is available in the lock-screen media controls.
- **Bottom bar:** slide your finger along it to pick a tab.

### Fixed
- **Shake to shuffle and face-down to pause now work with the screen off or locked** while music plays. In a pocket, shakes are ignored; with the screen off it takes two firm shakes. Face-down only counts when the phone lies flat.
- **Shuffle follows Phone / Cloud / Both** in Library, and now shuffles your whole library instead of 500 songs. Search has the same filter, and Recently added follows it too.
- **Widgets work reliably:** play from a widget even when the app is closed, no more stuck or outdated songs, and taps no longer stop the player.
- Backups now include the newer settings (haptics, gestures, Focus, lofi, noise, sleep, screensaver and more) and your Focus and garden history.
- A tip in Focus and Party helps keep Samsung from pausing LMusic in the background.
- The Stats tab is now just called Stats.
