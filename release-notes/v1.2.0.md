> [!TIP]
> **Installing this update keeps everything.** Download `LMusic-1.2.0.apk` below on your phone, open it and tap **Update**. Your library, playlists, listening stats, settings and edited tags stay exactly as they are — it installs on top of the version you have.

LMusic 1.2 — a new Home, smarter listening and a lot more polish.

### What's new
- **New Home:** Continue listening, Your mix, Made for you mixes, Recently played, Recently added, This week and Your top artists. The old collage and Daily Mix sections are in Settings › Appearance › "Show classic Home sections".
- **Redesigned everywhere:** Library, Search, album, artist and playlist pages, queue, sleep timer, song menu, tag editor and autofill now match the Yuyu design.
- **Now playing:** smaller artwork and controls, a sleep timer button, and a ⋮ button with the full song menu.
- **Haptics:** refined vibrations for play/pause, skips, seeking (a tick every 10 seconds), queue dragging, likes, toggles and more. Settings › Behavior › Haptics: Off / Subtle / Full.
- **Music folders:** choose the folders to include, instead of folders to exclude. If you're updating, your current music folders were picked for you — check them in Settings › Library › Music folders.
- **Update alerts:** LMusic checks GitHub once a week and notifies you when a new version is out. Turn it off in Settings › About.
- **Smart shuffle:** spreads artists out and pushes songs you just heard to the end (Settings › Playback).
- **Library health:** find songs with missing artwork, year, album artist or genre, plus duplicates, and fix empty tags in one go with batch autofill (Settings › Library › Library health). It only fills empty fields — it never replaces your tags.
- **Recaps:** tap Weekly recap or Monthly recap on the Stats tab for swipeable, shareable cards.
- **Widgets:** redrawn in the new style.
- **Smart features (experimental, off by default):** a small on-device model tags each song's mood and genre, then builds mixes (daily mixes, Calm, Upbeat, Focus, Workout, Late night, Discover), "Play similar" song radio, mood insights on Stats and a moods card in recaps. Nothing leaves your phone. Indexing runs only while charging, idle and above 50% battery. Turn it on in Settings › Developer › Smart features.
- The notification now shows the LMusic logo.

**If you use the widget or a Quick Settings tile** and it stopped working after 1.1, add it again.
